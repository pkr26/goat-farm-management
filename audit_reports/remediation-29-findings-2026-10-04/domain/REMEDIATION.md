## Accounting and rollout boundaries

- Disposal uses a consistent **exit-before-current-month-depreciation** convention. At-cost same-month sale has zero depreciation, full basis derecognition, zero gain/tax and zero residual book. Later sale balances prior depreciation plus remaining disposal charge to original cost. The model calculates a modeled tax base, not jurisdiction-specific tax law.
- Foundation stock retains the prior month-0 project-cost policy; in-run capitalized purchases alone belong to this account. Proportional cohort exits allocate basis within sex; max-age exits use exact age slots. A partial ordered buck sale now also reduces the count of same-month purchases protected from rotation, keeping the biological and book ledgers consistent.
- The migration refuses contradictory legacy acquisition/birth dates with an explicit reconciliation error. It does not invent historical dates or silently alter source facts. A real deployment with such legacy rows must reconcile them from records before upgrading.
- The SQL aggregation fixes complete expense history; other pre-existing bounded calibration samples (e.g. animal/kidding histories) retain their documented limits. Calibrated price bounds remain explicit model bounds rather than a promise to fit arbitrary outliers.
- Clinical accuracy is not established: screening tests validate evidence propagation/reviewability with synthetic provider contracts, not veterinary detection quality.
- Parent owns generated OpenAPI/frontend types, UI P&L row integration, full integrated suites and independent peer review. Those results supersede or complement this scoped validation.
